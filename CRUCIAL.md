LISTING RULES AND CONTENT GUIDELINES

To maintain a safe and trustworthy marketplace, all users must follow these rules.

1. Allowed Listings

You may list:

Legal physical goods

Used items

Collectibles

Electronics, vehicles, home goods, etc.

All items must belong to the seller or be legally allowed for resale.

2. Prohibited Items

The following items cannot be listed under any circumstances:

Illegal or Controlled Items

Weapons (firearms, ammunition, explosive materials)

Drugs, narcotics, tobacco products

Stolen or counterfeit goods

Wildlife products prohibited under Nepalese law

Government property

Sensitive or Regulated Items

Medicines, medical equipment requiring prescription

Alcohol

SIM cards, unverified digital accounts

Cryptocurrency trading

Harmful or Misleading Content

Scam listings

Listings designed to harvest user data

Pornographic content

3. Listing Requirements

Every listing must include:

Accurate title and description

Real photos of the actual item

Clear indication of the item’s condition

Honest and non-deceptive claims

Misleading or manipulated images are not allowed.

4. User Conduct

Sellers must:

Respond honestly to inquiries

Remove sold items promptly

Not artificially inflate bids or prices

Buyers must:

Make inquiries respectfully

Not place fraudulent offers

5. Reporting Violations

Users may report suspicious or illegal listings. The Platform reserves the right to review, hide, or remove any listing at its discretion.

6. Platform Rights

We may:

Modify listing rules

Remove harmful listings

Suspend repeat violators

Cooperate with Nepalese authorities when necessary
